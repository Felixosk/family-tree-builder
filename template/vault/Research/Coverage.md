## Five questions before every new book (copy them into the research note)
1. Can this record type answer the question at all?
2. Is it the right parish or office for the place (chapels, mother parish, renamed places)?
3. Which years are we really missing? Read only those.
4. Is there a register or an index? Then use that first.
5. Is the book already listed above?
Then: a sample of 20 images. If the place or name never appears: ask, do not read on.
